# PageSift 1.5.5 — 2026-11-16

Closing release on the `1.5.x` Loop line, completing long-horizon synchronization scheduling, adaptive acquisition and forgetting, observation reliability, contextual graph evidence, and cross-surface Loop verification.

## Changes

### Sync Scheduling & Co-Change Propagation
- **Non-aliased periodic detection & Poisson fallback (`pagesift-stat::regularity`, `pagesift-plan`)**: Detects regular site update intervals only when intermediate unchanged probes or server timestamps narrow the ambiguity window, avoiding false periodicity on coarse aliased polls (`detected_period` stays empty) and automatically falling back to Poisson rates when early changes or missed probes invalidate the cycle.
- **Hierarchical per-resource change learning & Lagrange budget water-filling (`pagesift-store`, `pagesift-engine/src/sync/scheduler.rs`)**: Tracks per-resource `ResourceLearnStats` blended with family priors via Gamma-Poisson shrinkage and allocates budget-constrained check intervals `T_j*` across active resources.
- **Hub/Feed co-change propagation & accurate multi-wave due counts (`pagesift-engine/src/sync/reconcile.rs`, `runner.rs`)**: When a monitored hub or feed observes a substantive update, outbound linked active children are pulled forward within `[min_interval_secs, max_interval_secs]` without skipping checks when unchanged; `SyncTickStats.candidates_due` accumulates across all disjoint waves within a tick (`co_changed_triggered`, `probe_recoveries`, `uncertain_observations` added with `#[serde(default)]`).

### Adaptive Acquisition, Forgetting & Controlled Transfer
- **`HttpWithEmbeddedJson` enabled during Sync HTTP probes (`pagesift-api`, `pagesift-engine`)**: Sync HTTP strategies set an internal `#[serde(skip)] try_embedded_json` option so thin client-rendered shells carrying `__NEXT_DATA__`, `__NUXT_DATA__`, or JSON-LD synthesize Markdown directly without launching browsers.
- **Modality-separated costs, browser shadow pricing & `DirectBrowser` recovery (`pagesift-plan`, `pagesift-engine`)**: `FamilyStats` separately tracks 304, HTTP, and browser latencies plus browser failure rates; `select_acquisition_strategy` prices browser escalations by remaining tick budget and live `RenderStats::tab_utilization()`, offloads marginal browser cases under high pressure, and schedules bounded `HttpVerifyBrowser` probes out of sustained `DirectBrowser` runs to recover when sites move from CSR to SSR.
- **Bayesian surprise forgetting (`pagesift-plan/src/experience_rates.rs`, `experience.rs`)**: `BetaSnapshot` and `ChangeRateSnapshot` accelerate decay when observations strongly refute prior rates (`calm <-> burst` and `SSR <-> SPA`).
- **Tenant-isolated cold-start transfer & negative-transfer rejection (`pagesift-plan/src/experience_transfer.rs`)**: Uses `pagesift_stat::shrink::beta_prior` across mature same-tenant peers on the same host/path skeleton with bounded prior weight (`<= 2.5`) and rolls back to pure local counts when independent local checks contradict the seeded prior (`tenant` isolation strictly enforced).

### Observation Reliability & Contextual DataGraph Evidence
- **Uncertain decode, truncation & invalid-304 censoring (`pagesift-engine/src/sync/capture.rs`, `inventory.rs`, `reconcile.rs`)**: Parses `decode:` fallback/replacement warnings, `body_truncated`, `graph_truncated`, and `not_modified:` warnings; censors `http_sufficient`/`substantive_change` and `Learner` updates on unreliable captures, preserves prior validators/baselines, and retains previously `Active` claims as `Unverified` instead of withdrawing them.
- **Contextual currency & `"UNKNOWN"` disambiguation (`pagesift-graph/src/norm.rs`, `corroborate.rs`, `builder.rs`)**: Resolves currency-less numeric prices against sibling `priceCurrency` fields, entity-group currencies, or unambiguous document currencies; assigns `"UNKNOWN"` rather than defaulting silently to `"USD"` when no currency cue exists, surfacing `UnitOrCurrencyMismatch` across conflicting currencies while keeping explicit same-page USD/numeric matching intact.
- **Persisted secondary citations & `SourceStateView.fact_clusters` (`pagesift-store`, `pagesift-engine`, `pagesift-mcp`)**: `StoredProvenance` persists `citations: Vec<CitationEdge>` across commits; `GraphStore` and `SyncStore` pass persisted citations into lineage conflict checks and populate `SourceStateView.fact_clusters` (`#[serde(default)]`).

## Measured Verification (Local Controlled & Archived-Corpus Evaluation)

Evaluated via `cargo run --profile quick -p pagesift-engine --example loop_eval` on local HTTP origins and 16 locally archived HTML pages (`benchmarks/data/showdown/d1/html`):
- **Periodic vs. Fixed/Poisson (600s site cycle over 18,000s simulated horizon)**: Mean detection lag decreased from `206.0s` (Poisson-only) to `63.8s` (non-aliased regularity; lower is better) using 33 checks vs. 300 under fixed 60s polling.
- **Next.js `__NEXT_DATA__` SSR synthesis (5 CSR test pages)**: Browser escalations reduced from `5/5` (`try_embedded_json` off) to `0/5` (`try_embedded_json` on; lower is better).
- **Tight-budget Lagrange water-filling (20 mixed hot/cold resources)**: Expected weighted missed-change hazard dropped from `724.41` (uniform interval) to `25.87` (water-filling; lower is better).
- **Controlled negative transfer check**: Misleading sibling prior incurred `2` local probe ticks before triggering negative-transfer rejection (`rejected = true`), after which posterior HTTP sufficiency dropped to `0.152`.
- **Archived real-web corpus (`16` pages × `3` sync ticks)**: `51` requests executed, `28` HTTP 304 conditional hits across ticks 2–3, `854` claims stored, `975 ms` wall time.
