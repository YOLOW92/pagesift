# Mixed benchmark (self-built), 2026-10-27

Rules, seeds and frozen sample lists: `benchmarks/mix/RULES.md` (frozen at f1a0cdf3). All tools run with default parameters (the only common change: ask for Markdown where a tool can produce it). Machine: i7-10700 (8C/16T), 15 GB RAM, GTX 1070. *Score with failures = 0*: every sampled item counts, an item a tool did not finish (timeout, crash, out of memory, empty output) scores 0. *Common items*: only items every compared tool finished. Raw per-item results: `benchmarks/mix/results/quality-<subset>.json`.

## 2. Fetching (crawler tools on a local replay of the A2 snapshots)

| Tool | Version | Pages | Completion ↑ | Failures ↓ | Timeouts ↓ | Pages with mojibake ↓ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **pagesift** | pagesift 0.80.0 | 269 | 0.937 | 17 | 0 | 1 |
| spider | spider 2.53.9 | 269 | 0.970 | 8 | 0 | 1 |
| Scrapy | 2.19.0 | 269 | 0.981 | 5 | 0 | 3 |
| Crawl4AI | 0.9.5 | 269 | 0.926 | 18 | 2 | 1 |

No content-quality score here (Scrapy returns raw HTML, the others whole-page Markdown). pagesift refuses pages it identifies as access barriers (4 of its 5 errors: captcha / challenge / block pages) instead of returning the barrier as content; that counts as a failure here. pagesift needs `--allow-cidr 127.0.0.1/32` to fetch from the loopback replay server (its SSRF guard blocks private addresses by default).

**Speed — fetching the 269 A2 pages from the replay server** (quiet machine, one tool at a time under the shared bench lock)

| Tool | p50 ms/item ↓ (1 worker) | p95 ms/item ↓ | items/s ↑ (1 w) | items/s ↑ (4 w) | items/s ↑ (16 w) | peak MB/worker ↓ | workers actually live at max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **pagesift** | 17.3 | 1551.3 | 2.7 | 8.0 | 14.5 | 406 | 16 |
| spider | 4.3 | 16.0 | 152.8 | 488.2 | 631.5 | 40 | 16 |
| Scrapy | 6.8 | 9.3 | 82.4 | 211.6 | 127.1 | 155 | 16 |
| Crawl4AI | 929.7 | 4310.0 | 0.6 | 2.4 | 4.2 | 926 | 13 |

pagesift is behind here. It runs with its default `auto` engine as one CLI process per page: the median page takes 17 ms, but the p95 is 1.5 s and the peak memory 406 MB, which suggests some pages triggered browser rendering; this release does not investigate further. The replay server is a small Python `ThreadingHTTPServer`; at 16 workers it is the bottleneck for the fastest tools (Scrapy is slower at 16 than at 4 workers), so the 16-worker column understates spider and Scrapy.

**Speed — PDF, same 144 pages (B1 10 % subset) for every tool** (quiet machine, one tool at a time under the shared bench lock)

| Tool | p50 ms/item ↓ (1 worker) | p95 ms/item ↓ | items/s ↑ (1 w) | items/s ↑ (16 w) | peak MB/worker ↓ | workers actually live at max |
