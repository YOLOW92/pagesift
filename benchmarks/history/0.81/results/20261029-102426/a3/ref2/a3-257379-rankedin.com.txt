Why Rankedin?

RankedIn offers many features that will stimulate maximum activity when it comes to your sport or your club:

LIVE STREAMING

Crete a live video with live result directly to your FB page or YouTube channel only by using your mobile device. Simple as that. Check E-referee to understand how it works.

Learn more
LIVE SCORING

Every player or admin can be a referee. Open the e-referee in your profile, find the game and start entering the points as they are scored. As you enter the points, they will also be updated on the live video or the live scoreboards.

Learn more
LIVE SCOREBOARDS

A simple link with the live score of one game, whole tournament, or a specific court is now available to you. Simply copy the link and add it on your TV next to the court, on the reception, on your FB page or anywhere you choose.

Learn more
Organization page

Every organization has a personal page with personal logo, contact details, address, rankings… Players will visit your organization’s page to find out about new events or check the past events organized by this organization. This will also allow any player to organize private challenges and small tournaments for your organization.

Learn more
