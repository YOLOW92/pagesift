# PDF → Markdown, olmOCR-bench (2026-10-22)

Data: [olmOCR-bench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/olmOCR-bench) (ODC-BY), the five subsets made of
born-digital PDFs: `arxiv_math` (522 pages, 2,927 formula tests), `headers_footers` (266 pages), `long_tiny_text`
(62 pages), `multi_column` (231 pages), `table_tests` (188 pages). Scored with the benchmark's own code
(Apache-2.0); the math subset with `bench_fast.py`, which gives the same pass/fail for every test (see README).
Every tool runs with its default settings, one page per file, on an Intel i7-10700 (8 cores, 16 threads), 15 GB RAM.

All scores are pass rates in percent; **higher is better**. Time is the mean per page, single thread; **lower is better**.

| Tool | Overall ↑ | math ↑ | table ↑ | order ↑ | present ↑ | absent ↑ | ms / page ↓ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **pagesift 0.12** | **71.0** | **44.3** | 56.1 | 61.2 | 35.7 | **68.0** | **3.4** |
| pymupdf4llm 1.28.2 (+ pymupdf_layout model) | 64.4 | 0.0 | **60.3** | **66.6** | 33.7 | 37.2 | 320 |
| pypdf 6.21.1 | 58.2 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 49.4 | **44.8** | 40.8 | 50 |
| MarkItDown 0.1.8 (pdfminer.six) | 57.3 | 0.0 | 19.8 | 39.3 | 31.2 | 38.4 | 200 |
| pdfplumber 0.11.10 | 50.4 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 10.0 | 7.7 | 41.8 | 124 |

"Overall" is the mean of the five subsets' scores as `benchmark.py` reports them (each subset's score averages its
test types, including the per-page baseline test). 23 of the 62 `long_tiny_text` pages are scanned images with no
text layer, which no text extractor can pass.

## Tuning and hold-out halves

Pages are split by `sha1(pdf path)` into a tuning half and a hold-out half (`diff.py` prints both). Design choices were
made looking at the tuning half and kept only when the hold-out half did not get worse.

| Subset (pass rate ↑) | before, tune | before, hold | after, tune | after, hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| table | 0.0 | 0.0 | 55.1 | 57.3 |
| order | 62.4 | 59.7 | 63.0 | 59.2 |
| present | 36.5 | 35.1 | 37.0 | 34.7 |
| absent (with the page-furniture rule) | 67.9 | 67.8 | 67.7 | 68.4 |

"Before" is pagesift with text extraction only (no tables, no formulas). The hold-out order and present rates moved
by 2 and 1 tests out of 424 and 242.

## What changed

- **Tables** (0 → 56%): ruled grids with pdfplumber's lattice algorithm (MIT, ported), booktabs tables between
  adjacent horizontal rules, framed boxes, and borderless tables found from runs of rows holding several short
  fragments; columns from gaps counted over the body rows only; wrapped cells merged back; prose, lists, tables of
  contents and chart labels rejected.
- **Formulas** (0 → 44%): the built-in encoding of embedded Type1/CFF fonts is read (TeX's math fonts often carry
  neither /Encoding nor ToUnicode), TeX glyph names are mapped to Unicode, and runs set in math fonts are written as
  `$…$` LaTeX with sub- and superscripts from glyph size and baseline, accents, `\mathbb`/`\mathcal`/`\mathfrak`,
  function names and TeX commands for symbols. Display formulas with fractions and limits are not rebuilt yet.
- **Page furniture**: a header or footer isolated in the top or bottom margin of a single page is dropped.
